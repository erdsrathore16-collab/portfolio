Chapter 02 — The Craft

What I bring to the table

01
Product Thinking
I don't just design screens — I design outcomes. Every decision is weighed against business goals, user needs, and technical reality.
02
Design Systems
Scalable systems that grow with the product. Tokens, components, documentation — the infrastructure that makes design sing at scale.
03
User Research
Contextual inquiry, usability testing, analytics synthesis. I believe in the radical act of actually talking to users — and more importantly, listening.
04
Dev Collaboration
I speak developer. Handoffs that are so clear, they're basically love letters. Reduced iteration cycles, fewer surprises, happier teams.
05
Discovery & Strategy
From zero-to-one problem framing to backlog prioritisation. I help teams ask better questions before rushing to answers.
06
Mentoring & Leadership
Growing junior designers, facilitating design crits, building design culture. The best work happens when the whole team levels up together.

Chapter 05 — The Human

The designer
who wanders
to wonder.

When I'm not crafting pixels and flows, you'll find me under the hood of a vintage motorcycle I'm slowly bringing back to life. There's something meditative about restoring old machines — stripping everything down to first principles, understanding how each part serves a purpose. Sounds familiar, doesn't it?

I camp. I offroad. I take photographs of things most people walk past. And I tell stories that make people laugh — because life's too short for boring presentations or po-faced design reviews. Good vibes are non-negotiable.
